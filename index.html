About Cycle Calendar

A menstrual calendar built to be quiet, private and predictable to use.

What it does

Log the days you bleed with a flow level, add symptoms, mood, a note and optionally your basal temperature. The calendar draws your logged periods, predicts the next ones from your own averages, and shades the estimated fertile window, ovulation day and PMS window.

Insights shows completed cycle lengths, your shortest and longest cycle, the variation between them, the next three predicted periods and the symptoms you log most often.

Privacy

Everything you enter stays in this browser's local storage on this device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third party request at runtime. The app is a single HTML file that works offline once loaded.

The only way data leaves the browser is if you export a backup file yourself or print the summary. Clearing site data, or using a private window that you then close, removes everything.

How predictions work

The average cycle length is the mean distance between period starts, using your last six completed cycles. The average period length is the mean duration of your last six periods. If you have fewer than two cycles, the defaults from Settings are used instead.

Estimated ovulation is placed the luteal phase length before the predicted next period, and the fertile window covers the five days before it plus the day itself. Your logged days always win over any estimate.

Not a medical device

Cycle Calendar is a personal tracking tool, not a contraceptive method and not a diagnosis. Cycles vary with stress, illness, travel and medication. Talk to a clinician about contraception, unusual bleeding, cycles shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days, or a period that stops for three months or more.

Built to last

Apache 2.0 licensed, no build framework, no dependencies at runtime, no cookies. The whole app is one HTML file plus a manifest and a service worker for offline use.
